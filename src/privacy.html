1. Who we are

The Centre of Foreign Learning (“CFL”, “we”, “us”) is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and the CFL learning portal (together, the “Site”). We handle personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

Contact: cfllearning33@gmail.com or 0722 840 989.

3. Who we share it with

  • Our staff. Administrators can see applications and accounts. Instructors can see the progress and writing of the students they teach. Students can see only their own information.
  • Google. We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Hosting) to store account and learning data and to serve the Site.
  • EmailJS. Delivers verification emails.
  • Google Maps. The home page shows an embedded map. When it loads, Google receives your IP address and browser details and may set its own cookies. Our own pages do not load Google Fonts; fonts are served from this Site.
  • Authorities. We will disclose information when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

4. Processing outside Kenya

Our providers may process data on servers outside Kenya. We choose providers that publish security and data-protection commitments, and we share only what the service needs.

5. How long we keep it

We keep application and learning records while you are a student, and afterwards only for as long as we need them for our records, for references or certificates you may ask for, and to meet legal obligations. When we no longer need them we delete or anonymise them. If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. Keeping it safe

The Site is served over HTTPS. Access to data is limited by role and enforced by database security rules, and sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. No online system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

7. Children

Many of our students are under 18. We ask for a parent or guardian’s details on every application so we can contact them about the application and the student’s safety. We use a child’s information only to provide their education and look after them, and we never use it for advertising. A parent or guardian may contact us at any time about their child’s data.

8. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. To work, the Site stores a few things in your browser:

  • Your sign-in. If you tick “Keep me signed in on this device”, it stays after you close the browser; if not, it ends when you close it.
  • The position of the navigation highlight while you move between portal pages (cleared when you close the tab).

You can clear this at any time in your browser settings. Google Maps, if loaded, may add its own cookies (see section 3).

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask us to tell you what we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, delete it, stop or limit how we use it, or move it to another service. Where we rely on your consent you may withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, email cfllearning33@gmail.com. We may need to confirm who you are first, and we will respond within the time the law requires.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya at odpc.go.ke.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If the change matters to you, we will tell you in the portal or by email.
